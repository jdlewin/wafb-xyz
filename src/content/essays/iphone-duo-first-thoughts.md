The iPhone Duo is a first-generation foldable that trades camera quality and outer screen utility for the novel experience of having a truly large inner screen that Apple has rethought from the ground up for a foldable form factor. Early reviewers settle on a consistent observation: this is a device for early adopters willing to embrace its particular vision, not a straightforward upgrade path for existing iPhone users.
